# Troubleshooting Log

## Issue 1: Duplicate account warning during lead conversion
- **Problem:** While converting Rahul Verma, Zoho showed a message that an account with a similar name already existed.
- **Cause:** Priya's lead and Rahul's lead both had the company name "self". Zoho found a match.
- **Fix:** I chose "Add to existing Account" so no second account was created.
- **Lesson:** Read the options on a warning before clicking, because creating a new account would have made a duplicate.

## Issue 2: Lead conversion finished without a Deal
- **Problem:** After converting Rahul, only an Account and a Contact were created, with no Deal.
- **Cause:** The Deal was not created during the conversion.
- **Fix:** I created the Deal manually and linked it to the Account and Contact.
- **Lesson:** After converting a lead, check the Deals tab to confirm the Deal exists.
